## Wildlife Guide Author Terms
#### Version 2026-10-virtual-guides-v1 · Effective 20 October 2026

These terms apply when you sell a Wildlife Guide on AnimalDex. They sit alongside the [AnimalDex Terms of Use](/legal/terms).

### 1. Who can sell

You must be 18 or older and in good standing on AnimalDex. You confirm your age when you accept these terms.

### 2. What you sell

A Wildlife Guide is advice built from your own sightings: your notes, answers to buyers' questions, and introductions to people you met. You are not guiding anyone in person, and AnimalDex does not organise tours. Keep your guide accurate and honest; never promise sightings.

### 3. Answering buyers

When someone buys your guide you must reply in their chat within 48 hours. If you don't, the buyer is refunded and your guide is paused until you turn it back on.

### 4. Wildlife and the law

Never share exact locations or times for threatened species, never encourage disturbing, feeding, touching or chasing wildlife, and respect park rules, permits and local laws.

### 5. Contacts

Only share people's contact details with their permission. Arrangements between a buyer and anyone you introduce are between them; AnimalDex is not a party to them.

### 6. Refunds

Buyers can ask for a refund within 7 days. Refunded purchases are not paid to you.

### 7. Payment

You receive 70% of each sale after app-store or card fees, added to your Earnings 8 days after the purchase if it was not refunded. Payouts follow the Earnings terms. You are responsible for your own taxes.

### 8. Removal

AnimalDex may hide a guide or stop you selling if a guide breaks these terms or the AnimalDex Terms of Use. Reversed payments after payout may be recovered from your Earnings.

### 9. Changes

We may update these terms; you will be asked to accept a new version before your guides can be sold again.
