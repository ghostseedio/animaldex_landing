SEO Notes

Use descriptive filenames because Google Images can read the filename context.

Good:

octopus-intelligent-decentralization.webp

Bad:

IMG_3921.webp

Alt text should describe the image naturally and include the concept only when relevant.

Good:

Octopus moving across the ocean floor, representing intelligent decentralization, camouflage, and distributed animal intelligence

Bad:

Octopus animal symbolism animal frequency AnimalDex octopus meaning animal lesson

Do not keyword-stuff alt text.
