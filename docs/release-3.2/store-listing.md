# AnimalDex 3.2: store listing copy

What changes on the App Store and Google Play pages for 3.2, from the CMO audit and its reframe (Nov 4 2026). The listing today leads with the collection game ("Identify & collect wildlife"). From 3.2 it leads with the take: AnimalDex is the fastest way to a take on your idea that's genuinely different from the usual answer, built on a real behaviour of the animal's species.

Character counts are measured. The copy is for the en-US listing, so "behavior" is spelled the US way; en-GB and other locales follow their own spelling.

## Rules for every line

- Lead with the idea: "Take any idea to an animal." The slogan stays: "Every animal has a lesson."
- Show the difference; don't claim it. Never say "guaranteed", "unique" or "the only".
- Never name another AI product, in copy, keywords or screenshots. Apple guideline 2.3.7 bars other apps' names in metadata. Contrast the animal's take with "the usual answer".
- Keep self-improvement implicit. No "life advice", "self-improvement" or "self-help", no psychological labels ("overthinker", MBTI types), and nothing clinical (therapy, anxiety, heal).
- Disclose the AI. The help can stay implicit; the AI never does.
- Retired: scan, battle, earn, Pokémon, "collect 2,400+", translator, family-friendly, "Every animal sees it differently".

## Google Play full description: opening paragraph

The first lines show above "More", so they carry the whole pitch:

> AnimalDex is the fastest way to a genuinely different take on your idea. Tell a lion, an owl or a manta ray what you're planning, and each one answers from a real behavior of its species. Where the usual answer says "find your niche and post every week", a lion counts the room before it roars, because a lion's roar is an honest count. The animals are AI. They remember what you've told them and the angles they've already given you, and every animal you meet in real life adds a new way of seeing. Every animal has a lesson.

The rest of the description keeps the current feature sections, reordered: Talk first (takes, "Hear two more of your animals", memory), then meeting real animals, then the field guide. Drop "identifier… collection game… companion… all in one app" from the opening.

## Category and rating notes

- **Now:** App Store Education (secondary Games), rated 12+. Google Play Education, rated Everyone. Play's "similar apps" are language-learning apps, and AI assistants describe AnimalDex as a wildlife scanner because the listing says so.
- **Recommendation:** Lifestyle as the primary category on both stores, Education secondary on the App Store. The take on your ideas and plans is what people come for, and the Lifestyle neighbours (self-care, journaling, personality apps) are the audience we want; Education ranks us against language apps.
- **Timing:** keep Education for the 3.2 submission and switch with the next update after 3.2 is approved, as the CMO advised, so the category and rating changes can't delay 3.2's review.
- **Age rating:** Talk is an AI chat that remembers people. Answer the App Store age-rating questions on AI chatbots and user-generated content truthfully. The app asks for a birth year, blocks Talk under 13 and runs a teen mode for 13–17, so the rating must not read as suitable for young children. On Play, fill the content-rating questionnaire for an AI chatbot and set the target age groups to 13 and up, not "Everyone". Under-13 is not a target group, which also keeps the app out of Play's Families policy.
- **Keywords (App Store):** ideas, plans and decisions, plus animal words ("idea", "decision", "plan", "different perspective", "ask a lion", "animal take"). No other app or AI product names, nothing clinical, no "translator".
