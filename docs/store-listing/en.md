## 4. Full description (max 1,200)

```
Ask any animal anything, and it answers from how its kind really lives.

Got an idea, a plan or a decision? Take it to a lion, an owl or a manta ray. Each answers as itself, from a real behavior of its species, in a few clear sentences.

Not sure yet? Bring in two more of your animals. A short report shows which way fits you best.

When it helps, an animal suggests one small step. The first step you finish together earns its Power.

Your animals remember you and sometimes message you first. Every idea you work through lands in your Diary.

Photograph or film a real animal: AnimalDex identifies it and adds it to your collection with its field guide. Replies can be read aloud in the animal's own voice.

THE ARK
When a step works, that animal comes aboard a shared Ark. Animals aboard eat, breed and age based on each animal's field guide. Your Ark Score counts what your steps bring, from 0 to 100.

Pro is optional; eligible new yearly subscribers start with a free week.

Every animal has a lesson.

The animals are AI characters and can be wrong, as can identifications. Ages 13 and up. Keep a safe distance from wildlife.
```
