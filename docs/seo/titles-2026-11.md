# Answer-first titles, November 2026

Search Console, 13 Nov 2026: the tier-list pages below sit at positions 7 to 9
with 0.3 to 0.6% CTR, and three comparison pages at 0.1 to 0.2%. Every title now
names the real #1 (read from the page's ranking data, never invented), carries a
number, and leads with the surprise. Every description ends "Ask the #1
yourself." Titles are under 60 characters, descriptions under 155. H1s and the
slogan are unchanged.

The #1 for each tier list is `getExpandedRankingEntries(page)[0]`, the first
row the page renders (the curated top ten leads every table). The comparison
"winner" is the page's `quickVerdict`; the scenario counts come from
`scenarioBreakdown`.

Where the copy lives: `src/data/rankings.ts` (`seoTitle`, `description`),
`src/data/quality-ranking-pages.ts` (loyal, gentle, patient),
`src/data/challenges.ts` (new `seoTitle` / `seoDescription`, read by
`src/app/[locale]/(composited)/comparisons/[slug]/page.tsx`). Pinned by
`src/lib/tier-list-seo.test.ts`, `src/lib/quality-rankings.test.ts`,
`src/lib/seo-ctr-titles-nov-2026.test.ts` and `src/lib/seo-locale-safety-gate.test.ts`.

## What to watch

Compare CTR on these twelve URLs in Search Console four weeks after deploy
(filter: page, 28 days, before and after). Position should not move; CTR is
the only number the change is meant to lift. If a title is rewritten by Google
in results, the H1 is the usual cause and it is unchanged here on purpose.
