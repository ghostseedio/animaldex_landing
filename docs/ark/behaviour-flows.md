## 0. The rule: no fake events (owner, 2026-11-23, binding)

> "no fake events": every found / caught / ate / near-miss step must be backed by something real.

| Step | Backed by (the engine's own record) | Without it |
|---|---|---|
| Caught X, stalk and pounce on X | `meal` event: its time, its prey (a real Ark animal) | never shown |
| Missed: X got away (near miss) | `escaped` event (predator in `otherAnimalId`): its time, its prey | never shown; no seeded misses, no "found nothing", no "gave up" |
| Eating its everyday food | the engine says it is fed (not hungry), **and** the food is its background food as the engine stores it (`voyage_species_traits.ambient_phrases`, 2,036 kinds; `data/ark/ambient-foods.json`). A fox eats insects and fruit (its engine background food), never "scraps" or a rodent from the diet sentence | hungry: searching, moving on, resting only |
| Hiding X for later; dug up its cache | SIG-EVENTS `signature` food_cache; `fed` source cache | never shown |
| Eating its keeper's food | kept (`keptFed`) and the keepers' feeding time | never shown |
| Displaying | SIG-EVENTS `signature` display / courtship, at its time | never shown; no seeded calls, scent marks, sparring, rivals or refusals |
| Courting then with its mate | `mated` | never shown |
| Building its nest (trips counted) | a clutch the server laid (`laid`) within the next 3 ticks, paired, in season | never shown |

- **Neutral steps only, where the engine records nothing:** searching (listening, watching, on its web, probing),
  moving on, resting, grooming, drinking, sleeping. They claim no outcome.
- **The engine's events win over its hours:** an event at a time its activity pattern says it sleeps wakes it two
  minutes before (the fox strip's 16:18 escape).
- **Live log moments are server events only** (caught, cached, near miss, dug up a cache): weight 1, none seeded.
- **Seeded from the animal's id and the engine's tick**: only durations, which spot, which of its background foods. The
  engine's day is `ARK_DAY_MS` of the shared clock (one Ark year in 14 real days, about 55 real minutes).
- Tested: `ark-flows.test.ts` "no fake events" runs every pilot kind fed, hungry and in season with no server events and
  finds no outcome step, no moment, and no food that is not its engine background food.

## 1. What we have today, and why it reads wrong

- `behaviour-schedule.ts` cuts waking time into **80 s slots** (`SLOT_SECONDS`), one goal a slot (`slotPlanAt`).
- The card (`animal-status.ts`) says one activity a slot. "Looking for food" never becomes "eating", and nothing ever
  succeeds, misses, gives up or saves food.
- The live log speaks only when a slot's goal changes.
- What must stay: the schedule is a pure function of the shared clock and the id, the same for every viewer, cheap
  (memoised trips) and visual only.

## 2. The model: steps with outcomes, inside the engine's tick

```
TICK     = (the engine's Ark day)  its steps from the tick's start to its end, seeded hash32("flow-tick", id, tick)
BOUT     = SLEEP | FORAGE | HUNT_TO(kill) | ESCAPE(escaped) | CACHE(saved) | KEPT_FED | DRINK | REST | GROOM | REPRO
SLEEP    = settle(roost) · sleep · wake                      (awake by its activity pattern; interrupted by a predator)
FORAGE   = fed:    (search(mode) | move on) · eat(background food) ... [· dance] · digest
           hungry: (search(mode) | move on)* · move on · rest            (no seeded tries, misses or finds)
HUNT_TO  = search · stalk · attempt · CATCH(kill.at, prey) · (eat | cache when saved) · digest
ESCAPE   = search · stalk · attempt · near-miss(escaped.at, prey)                         (the engine's own miss)
KEPT_FED = wait for the keeper · eat its keeper's food (hay, pellets, seed, meat, fish)    (at the keepers' times)
DRINK    = to water · wary at the edge · drink                    (kinds that get their water from food: no bout)
REST     = rest | digest (after a meal, longer for a big one) | bask | shelter from heat
REPRO    = section 5
```

**Each step** is `{kind, from, to, pose, food, detail, server}`.

- `pose` is `moving`, `air` or `still`. Eating, catching, caching, handling and mating are always `still`, never
  airborne and never walking.
- `server: true` marks a step that is the engine's own event.

**How a bout is chosen.** On a tick the engine has it fed it eats its background food at least once. Then the next server event's lead-in:
the kill's hunt, a cache, an escape, the keeper's feeding time or a mating. Every bout gives way to the event's
lead-in (`limit`). Then its rhythm (two peaks, siesta) and its season (a rutting stag barely eats). After a search that came to nothing it
moves on, rests or grooms before searching again. A drink is never repeated within 30 minutes.

**Durations** are draws around the species' own means (section 4), held to 0.25×–3× of the mean, with a search step at
least 20 s so the card never flickers. There is no fixed slot. The tick's end is where the engine re-decides, and only
there is a bout cut. It is a real boundary, the moment the next outcome is known.

**Cost.** A tick is 20–120 steps with a few draws each, about 0.05 ms. It is remembered per (id, tick, server
version), for the last 200 ticks read. The step now is a binary search. Bars and the day view read the same ticks. For
animals out of view nothing is worked out until the card or the day view asks.

## 3. Same for every viewer, and the server wins

- **Inputs** are the animal's id, the tick, the flow parameters, and the server's released events and state:
  - fed this tick (not hungry, or a `fed` event that day);
  - `meal` (its kills: time, prey name);
  - `escaped` (for the predator in `otherAnimalId`: time, prey name);
  - SIG-EVENTS `signature` food_cache, display and courtship (time, recipe);
  - `fed` with source cache;
  - `moved_to_mate` (ARK-KIN);
  - `mated`, its young, its kept status and its keepers' feeding times.
- **Re-plan.** An event released later changes the `version`, so the ticks from its release on are worked out again.
  The past someone already watched is not rewritten; the card log gains the server's line.
- **Parity tests** (`ark-flows.test.ts`): two viewers reading in a different order, after forgetting, and one arriving
  mid-tick all get identical steps. The flows also never catch without a kill, never eat on an unfed day, eat on a fed
  day, never mate without `mated` and never cache without a saved cache. A server escape is the near miss at its time,
  with its prey.

## 5. Reproduction flows (they wait until hunting is live and settled)

The server is the truth for pairs, clutches, births and deaths. Flows fill the time between and never contradict them.

```
SEEK    = call | scent-mark | roam toward ARK-KIN's mate home (moved_to_mate) | go to the lek        (in season, unpaired, wild)
MEET    = a candidate in reach [CONTEST]
CONTEST = spar | chase | call duel                    (no winner shown until the engine models one)
COURT   = display | gift | dance | song → turned down (normal) | MATE
MATE    = side by side, discreet, ONLY at the server's `mated`
NEST    = site · gather material · build | dig | scrape · defend           (in season, paired; never the clutch itself)
CARE    = incubate | gestate (change-overs as its offspring text says) · feed young (provision) · guard (guardYoung)
          · fledge | wean                             (only while the server has its eggs or young)
FAIL    = nest loss only on the server's death of eggs or young → re-nest | give up the season
```

| Parameter | From |
|---|---|
| mating system | stored text: "leks" → lek (sage-grouse, ruff, peafowl); "rut … contests" → rut (red deer); "both parents feed" / pair sociality → pair; `mass_spawner` → broadcast; `reproduction` (asexual, budding) → none |
| season | `voyage_species_traits.season` (301), as the engine's `seasonMonths`; none known: no seek or court bouts |
| display, contest | recipes: strut_display, court_display, court_dance, pushup; antler_clash, horn_clash, jaw_spar… |
| nest, incubation, who incubates | offspring text ("The hen scrapes a shallow nest under a sagebrush shrub … incubating alone"); props nest kinds |
| provision, guard | `provision` behaviour ("Both parents feed the nestlings"); guardYoung (engine) |

Rules:

- **Kept animals (KEPT-NO-HELP).** House, farm and zoo animals court only with a real partner of their kind, of the
  other sex, in the same tank, pen or enclosure. A neutered pet never courts or mates. They get no breeding help.
- **ARK-KIN.** "Looking for a mate" walks toward where `moved_to_mate` sent it, and never anywhere the engine is not
  sending it. With mate seeking off, seeking stays in its home range (calls, scent).
- **SIG-EVENTS.** A saved courtship or display plays at its time with its recipe.
- **Pilot.** The rut, the lek and a pair's nest. The honey bee's "waggle dance" is foraging talk (its stored text), not
  courtship, so the bee has no mating system in the flows.

## 6. Card and live log

- **Card "Now:"** is the step's line. Server-backed: "Stalking a Bank Vole", "Pouncing on a Bank Vole", "Caught a Bank
  Vole", "Missed: the Wood Mouse got away", "Hiding a Bank Vole for later", "Eating meat from its keeper",
  "Displaying". Background food: "Eating an insect", "Grazing · eating grass · patch 3", "Drinking nectar". Neutral:
  "Listening for prey", "Waiting on its web", "Moving on", "Resting", "Drinking".
- **A repeating cycle is one activity with progress** (coordinator, 2026-11-23: the sparrow's nest strip flickered
  between "Gathering nest material" and "Building its nest" every 20-40 s). Grazing, browsing, foraging, flower visits,
  filter-feeding and nest trips are grouped bouts (`FlowBout`): the card's line stays ("Building its nest", "Visiting
  flowers", "Grazing", "Foraging") and only its progress counts up ("trip 5, carrying grass", "14 so far", "eating
  grass · patch 3", "6 spots searched"). The steps inside still drive the pose.
- **The flicker rule** (`CARD_RETURN_SECONDS = 30`, tested over every pilot kind fed, hungry, in season, with kills and
  escapes, and a kept cat): a card line may not come back within 30 s when it alternates with one other line. Solo
  steps that sit between two of the same line (a meal between searches, a dance between flower bouts) last at least
  32 s; a bout never starts with under 90 s left before the tick's end or the server's next event (it carries on).
- **Card log** is its last steps, newest first. Repeats merge.
- **Live log moments are the server's events only** (section 0): caught, cached, near miss (`escaped`), dug up its
  cache. Always said (weight 1). The Ark-wide one line a 5 s window is unchanged.
- **Words** (built 2026-11-23): `ark.flow` in all 45 locale files, 64 keys (`FLOW_WORDS_EN` in `flows.ts` is the
  English copy), hand-written in the lane (no paid API), tested for every key and its `{n}`. `stepLineIn(step, words)`
  renders a language: English (`words` null) keeps its lines with the food named ("Caught a European Rabbit"); a
  translation names no food ("Hat seine Beute gefangen"), since the food words are the engine's English ambient phrases.
  Counts are written "spots searched: {n}" so no language needs plural forms.

## 7. The real costs of searching (engine; AnimalDex branch `flow-costs`, flag `flow_costs`, OFF)

Design only. Engine changes land one at a time after hunting goes live (about 25 Nov), each with the 5-seed sim.

- **Per engine step** (`encounter.ts` step, `STEPS_PER_ARK_DAY`), for each awake wild animal:
  - **search effort** `e`: 0 to 1, the share of the step spent searching. It rises with hunger and is the guild's
    share, from the same parameters as the flows.
  - **energy cost**: `e × searchCost(guild) × mass^0.75`, against the energy bar the engine keeps (thrive's condition).
    Searching costs more than resting, and a long fruitless search weakens it (it turns hungry sooner).
  - **time lost**: a step spent searching is not spent resting or breeding. Breeding readiness slows while effort is
    high.
  - **exposure**: `e × exposure(mode)` raises its chance of being chosen as prey in that step's encounter draw. Moving
    search is exposed; sit-and-wait and web are low.
- **Seeds**: its own `rngFor(..., 'flow-cost:<id>')`, so no other draw moves.
- **Flag off**: the engine is md5-identical to main in popsim (5 seeds).
- **The flows read the same effort.** A hungry, long-searching animal is shown searching longer. Effort is in the
  snapshot as the animal's `effort` for the step.
- **Real prey encounters, for near misses (owner, "no fake events").** Today the only real near miss is `escaped`. For
  near misses to be common enough to read as a hunt's drama, the engine must make them: in each step's encounter draw a
  searching predator meets a real prey animal of its food links in its zone; the encounter ends in a kill (`meal`, as
  now), an escape (`escaped`, payload `{preySpecies, cost}`), or nothing logged. Each encounter costs the predator
  energy (`attemptCost(guild) × mass^0.75`) and, for the prey, an exposure step (its flee: `flee-envelope.md`). Only
  logged encounters may be shown; the flows pin them as they pin `escaped` now.
- **Background feeding events (optional, for the log).** Ambient food feeds without an event today. If the owner wants
  "found / eating" moments in the live log for everyday food, the engine logs a light `foraged` record per animal per
  Ark day (`{food: ambient phrase}`), sampled, not stored per bite; until then the flows show everyday eating on the card
  only, never as a log moment.

## 8. Sequencing and what ships first

**The scene adapter (pilot, `src/ark-scene/flows/scene-adapter.ts`, switch `globalThis.__arkFlows`, harness `?flows=1`;
off by default, and nothing runs while off: the schedule tests pass unchanged).**

- A slot its flow spends at least 60% still (`FLOW_STILL_SHARE`: eating, resting, a catch, a still search) is a stay:
  the schedule's route for it is dropped, so it stands where it is. Other slots keep the schedule's route: a moving step
  is the schedule's trip leg. Where a slot ends the next begins (tested: no jump at any slot edge; `finishOf` looks back
  48 slots with flows on, a long graze being goal-less slots in a row).
- At a stay, its beats are the flow's: a meal is bite and chew (birds and insects peck), standing, head down; a still
  search looks round (`scan`); rest, drink and groom as themselves. The schedule's walked beats (`step`, `stalk`,
  `play`) are kept where they are.
- In the scene a flow sleeps and wakes with the schedule's own hours (the server's activity), never its own.
- The fed state is the server's condition (`hungry` or not); events come in through `setFlowServer`.
- **A flock reads one flow** (built 2026-11-23): a member's subject is seeded by its leader's id and is fed or hungry by
  its leader's condition and awake by its leader's hours (`flowSubjectFor`, `behaviour-schedule.ts`). Before, each
  member's own hunger or sleep built the shared memo, whichever came first, so a sparrow's stay was rewritten from a
  flow it was not in and stood in `pause`. The stay's beats also step at most to the flow step's end, so a short peck
  between searches is never inside one long beat's stride.
- **The meal's cache act** (`meal-acts.ts`, a cached kill, `?cache=1`): pick up (head down), carry it in its mouth to a
  spot 3 to 6 body lengths off, drop it beside it, dig (the rig's `dig` clip where it has one, else its graze; nose to
  the ground, a quick scratching rhythm), push it into the hole and cover it (a slower push), then rest there. The flow
  pins its kill, carry, cache and rest at the act's own times (`addFlowKill`), so the card reads "Caught", "Carrying …
  off to store it", "Hiding … for later", "Resting".
- In-scene pictures: `scripts/ark-flow-scene-shots.mjs` (flows on and off at the same moments, captioned with the
  flow's line, the pose's beat and today's card).
